# Material presets

Condensed adaptations of the owner-supplied 28-style prompt pack. Combine one
row with the geometry and construction rules in SKILL.md; no source-document
access is required during use. These are artistic treatments, not factual
scientific imagery. Background and lighting choices are preset-specific.
