# Distributed brand search

Select relevant third-party surfaces, publish useful brand content when authorized,
and measure whether it earns discovery, qualified use and accurate citations.
Read [the dated platform study](references/platform-study.md) for the starting
inventory, primary sources, indexing limits and experiment design. Refresh stale
eligibility before executing; the inventory is research, not account accreditation.

## Scope and selection

Read the owning product's current purpose, audience, lifecycle, public availability
and existing evidence. Internal/private tools can be exempt from public distribution;
never expose owner-only URLs, customer data or private assessments. A landing for an
unreleased prototype must say so. Preserve the product's canonical brand identity.

Separate three jobs: an authentic brand profile; an original answer, tutorial or
demonstration for a platform's audience; and syndication of an existing article.
Choose platforms for the audience and useful content format, not domain authority
alone. Prefer a small test on the best-fit channels over an identical batch across
all hosts. Do not mislabel brand-authored pages as independent endorsements.

Review an existing platform/account receipt before repeating checks. Record current
policy, exact host and URL form, public/logged-out access, indexing controls,
canonical handling, cost, account ownership and blocker. A reachable root domain or
an indexed older page does not establish that a new profile will index quickly.
Do not invent the missing twentieth item in the owner's nineteen-domain list.

## Content and execution

For each selected surface, map a real audience question to a supported answer and
relevant next action. Create destination-specific content: real workflow evidence,
examples, limitations and sources. Profiles give consistent identity and an accurate
canonical destination. Full syndication records source attribution and supported
canonical controls; a duplicate may consolidate to another canonical rather than
occupy an extra search result. Native original answers can target distinct needs.

Use existing publication workflows and the owner's authorized accounts. Research
and draft preparation do not authorize account creation, public posts or spending.
If a live distribution run is requested, honor that authorization without redundant
approval; resolve only missing identity, content, destination or cost decisions.
For a one-off launch, use the existing launch-campaign workflow rather than creating
a second campaign tracker. Do not manufacture engagement, irrelevant links, fake
reviews, duplicate low-value pages or independent-looking endorsements.

Before any public post, use the Humanizer skill when it is available. If it is
not listed with advertised skills, check the managed install at
`~/.local/share/agent-config/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md`. Review the rewrite
against the approved facts and preserve claims, links and disclosures; retain
the final copy revision. If the owner specifically requests Humanizer and it
cannot be loaded, hold that post and report the missing step.

## Indexing services

Verify exact eligible URLs and service mechanics before submission:

- Google Search Console requests need the appropriate verified property permissions.
  Use the current supported inspection/sitemap workflow for managed URLs. Its
  inspection API reports status; do not invent a general request-indexing API.
- Google's Indexing API is restricted to eligible job-posting/livestream pages.
  Ordinary profiles, product pages and articles are not eligible by adding fake schema.
- Prefer existing native IndexNow integration on eligible hosts. Manual submission
  requires a publicly verified key for the exact host and authorized URL scope.
  Controlling an account on a social host does not prove host/key control. IndexNow
  notifies participating engines; do not describe it as a Google submission.
- Evaluate a third-party service by supported engines, actual mechanism, URL rights,
  price, privacy and independently checkable results. Separate a submission/crawl
  promise or refund policy from a search engine's indexing decision. No vendor is
  accredited by this skill. Do not purchase an unspecified service.

Record service, URL, UTC submission time, response and receipt, without credentials.
A 200/202 or vendor dashboard saying success is submission evidence only. Check at
24 hours, seven days and thirty days as observation windows, not guaranteed SLAs.
Stop an unchanged failed submission from becoming a retry loop; record the blocker.

## Measurement and weekly cadence

Freeze a manageable query/prompt panel, market, language, device and engine/surface
before the pilot. Include brand lookup and neutral long-tail problem/comparison
questions separately. Record baseline, exact content URL, publication time and
controls/comparable queries where feasible. One product experiment at a time fits
the existing one-lever cadence; reuse its issue and assessment, not a new queue.

Keep these records separate:

1. Effort: qualified platform/account, original content, verified live URL,
   crawlability/canonical checks, authorized submission receipt and measurement wiring.
2. Results: indexing evidence per URL/engine with first-observed time; unique
   brand-controlled URLs in the first 10/20 organic results for the frozen panel;
   non-QA referrals and meaningful completed actions; independently captured AI
   mentions, actual cited URLs, answer accuracy and recommendations.

Record indexed / confirmed excluded / not observed / inaccessible / not checked.
An absent site: result is not definitive proof of exclusion. First-observed indexing
is an upper bound, not the engine's exact indexing timestamp. Preserve sample size,
missing captures, personalization and attribution limits. Denominators use the
observed eligible slots; organic results, ads and AI links are different surfaces.

A brand-authored citation is distinct from an independent editorial citation.
Report direct citation of a third-party URL separately from canonical-site citations,
and mentions separately from both. Do not turn result visibility into qualified
use, or generalize Google's retrieval rules to all LLM products.

At the weekly review, retain the baseline and observations, decide keep / improve /
stop, and name the next lever and follow-up date. Update the checklist's SEO and GEO
lanes and the existing private score evidence without changing history or awarding
Result points for publication. No universal top-20 threshold, 24-hour guarantee or
2027 forecast is established by the study.
