# Copy that earns its claims

Identify the audience's situation, the page/message job and the next useful
action. Use the positioning already established; ask only about a material gap.

Draft the actual copy. Prefer concrete capabilities and recognizable customer
language over adjectives. Connect a benefit to how the product delivers it.
Place proof near the claim it supports and disclose meaningful limitations.
Do not manufacture scarcity, urgency, testimonials, guarantees or comparative wins.

For page copy, make the opening explain the product, audience and reason to care;
then answer the objections relevant to the next action. This is an information
priority, not a required section template. Preserve the selected visual system.

For replies/outreach, show the incoming context and exact proposed text with
recipient/channel before an execution decision. Do not send from a writing request.

Finish with a recommendation and a small number of materially different wording
options only where useful. Label performance predictions as hypotheses. Review
for specificity and the owner's voice; human-sounding prose alone is not accuracy.
