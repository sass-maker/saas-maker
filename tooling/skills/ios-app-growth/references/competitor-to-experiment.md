# From competitor evidence to one experiment

Use for competitor teardown, organic creative research or a focused growth
decision. Keep established product, measurement and permission rules; this is
not a second growth system. For a non-iOS/non-subscription product, state the
adaptation and use its actual objective rather than importing a paywall model.

## Evidence and competitor patterns

Label claims observed, reported, hypothesis or unknown, attaching source and
inspection date. Analytics also need cohort, denominator and attribution window.
Screenshots prove visible layout, not causal revenue impact. Do not imply access
to an app, campaign or analytics account that was unavailable.

Inspect a bounded relevant set of competitors, with coverage limits rather than
a fixed quota. Capture customer/promise, payment evidence, complaints, acquisition
examples and the reason someone might choose our product instead. Competitor
payments can support category demand; they do not validate our switching case,
acquisition cost or retention. Similarities among winners are not causal proof.

For each useful pattern record:

> Observed behavior → rationale hypothesis → our concrete adaptation → observable test.

Where authorized, examine opening, onboarding, core action, result, return loop
and monetization. Record important omissions as well as features. Distinguish a
demo from the first useful result. Give each proposed onboarding step a user
question, necessary input, value delivered, next action and measurable event.
Prefer a focused intervention in the existing loop to rebuilding the app.

## Distribution research

For relevant public content, ads or creator examples record the source/date,
hook, first frame, when the product appears, demonstration, CTA and observable
results. Views and reactions do not establish installs, payments or retention.
Borrow the format through original execution, not assets, branding or invented proof.

Choose one channel test and a small, justified set of variants. Creator fit
depends on demonstrated content ability and audience fit, not followers alone.
Contact, production, publication and spend still need applicable authorization.
Do not start a content factory or prescribe short-form video to every product.

Organic learning before paid amplification is an option, not a required order.
Compare cash and labor costs of credible learning methods; a bounded paid-first
test can be reasonable. Include research, creative production, coordination and
review time, with an explicit valuation method, in fully loaded acquisition cost.
Keep media-only CAC separate. With zero acquired payers, CAC is undefined.
Use the existing measurement/economics reference for cohort value and payback;
avoid double-counting labor or fees and account for prepaid delivery obligations.

At a funnel drop-off inspect audience, acquisition promise, actual value, price
and immediate friction. The location of loss does not identify its cause.
Improve what strengthens the core promise: fewer steps or a better default may
beat another feature. Do not equate payment with evidence sufficient to scale.

## Compact deliverable

Lead with test / iterate / scale / stop / insufficient evidence and the reason.
If comparison helps, use at most five rows: pattern/source, concrete adaptation,
observable verification and what to avoid. Do not fill a quota.

Then specify one experiment:

- decision resolved; hypothesis and supporting evidence;
- audience and acquisition promise; actual baseline or unknown;
- single change/control and what stays fixed;
- primary outcome, denominator, cohort, source and attribution window;
- guardrails, cash cap, time cap and valuation of labor;
- proposed success threshold, stop condition and review point;
- owner and required authorization.

Mark unset budgets and unavailable baselines explicitly. If attribution is
missing, evidence collection may be the next experiment. Use adoption or
credibility outcomes when appropriate instead of forcing revenue metrics.
End with the one highest-leverage action, not a speculative backlog.
