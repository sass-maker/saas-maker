# One prospecting workflow

Use discovery methods as modes, not separate agents or production queues.
Start with listicles and competitor-link context plus a bounded check for lost
links and unlinked mentions. Add broken-link/outdated-guide research when useful.
Do not mistake a directory inventory for qualified opportunities or acquisition.

## Evidence gates by method

| Method | Required before qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Lost destination | Exact referring page still links to the old URL; old destination genuinely returns 404/410; replacement serves the original reader need. A redirect proposal needs review and, after authorized application, a complete chain test. No unrelated homepage fallback. |
| Unlinked mention | Open the page, save the exact mention sentence, inspect existing links, match a useful destination and verify a public editorial contact with its source. |
| Listicle | Confirm at least two actual competitors are recommended, the product is absent and verified functionality fills a reader need. If AI citations motivate discovery, retain dated engine observations rather than claiming a snippet proves citation. Flag payment/affiliate conditions. |
| Competitor backlink | Inspect the exact paragraph/link and why the competitor earned it; match an existing asset or capability. Classify editorial, sponsorship and business-relationship links separately. |
| Broken resource | Establish genuine unavailability, inspect the referring context and archived resource to reconstruct the task. Qualify only if the replacement completes it; missing archive evidence can block qualification. |
| Outdated guide | Cite a dated first-party announcement and the exact instruction that no longer works. Age or changed ownership alone is insufficient. |

403, 429, authentication, timeouts and blocked fetches are unverified, not dead.
Do not bypass access controls. Authority metrics are dated metadata, never an
acceptance rule; assess relevance, content quality and credible audience evidence.

## Single record and review queue

Use one stable prospect ID, with duplicate detection by exact source and proposed
destination (preserve query parameters where meaningful). Retain:

- exact source URL, evidence excerpt and observation timestamp;
- discovery method(s), reader benefit, proposed destination and product evidence;
- public contact and contact-source URL, commercial terms (including unknown);
- qualification decision, missing evidence and last verification date;
- draft/version, reviewer, authorized action scope and timestamp when applicable;
- status history, execution receipt, replies, costs and time spent.

Lifecycle: `discovered → qualified → draft_ready → approved → sent → live_verified`.
Allow `rejected`, `blocked`, `declined` and `lost` with reasons. Approval is for
the shown draft/action; `sent` requires a send receipt and `live_verified` requires
page inspection. Record inbound placements without inventing earlier outreach
states. Record replies separately—an editor's promise is not a live link.
If previously verified evidence goes stale or cannot be rechecked, preserve its
history and mark current verification unknown; do not silently count it as live.

This is a record contract, not authorization to persist provider data or to build
a database. Use an existing authorized product store; otherwise return a compact
review table. Raw private material never belongs in shared skill source.

## Draft and placement checks

Every draft states a concrete reader benefit supported by product evidence,
links the exact relevant contribution and identifies the verified editor contact.
Keep it for review; flag commercial conditions and do not negotiate or send
without authorization. Do not join exchange networks or offer compensation for
ranking links. Editorial relevance can justify a competitor link; no blanket ban.

For an acquired link inspect the published source, raw href, resolved destination,
anchor, surrounding text, raw rel attributes (including absent/multiple values),
commercial terms and verification time. Do not trust a vendor's dofollow label.
Keep recovered links separate from new ones. Count distinct referring sites
separately from placements, declaring the domain-grouping rule and handling
shared-hosting/subdomain ambiguity explicitly.

Report qualified prospects, reviewed pitches actually sent, replies and verified
placements, plus observed referral visits/useful product actions where available.
State attribution method and unknowns. Include time/money spent. Changes in search
visibility after a link do not prove causation; do not call these proposed metrics
demonstrated returns from an article or small selected sample.

## Conditional asset work

Require a named audience, specific task and identified distribution opportunities
before proposing asset production; building still needs user authorization.

- Free tools: identify likely citing pages first; test valid/invalid inputs,
  mobile behavior and actual output; assess runtime costs and secret handling.
- Statistics pages: define sample, exclusions and missing values; retain
  reproducible calculations, independently check public figures and disclose limits.
- Reporter requests: verify fit, eligibility, deadline and submission rules;
  only substantiated experience and real quotations qualify.
- Infographic/video contributions: require specific publisher interest and
  permission before production; verify labels/sources, use real product screens
  for demonstrations and disclose involvement. Source credit is optional, not a
  ranking-link condition in exchange for the contribution.
