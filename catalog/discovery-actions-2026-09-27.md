## What a million views would require

The target must specify **per product or portfolio total**, **owned-site human pageviews or cross-platform views**, and **the time window**. No current evidence supports a million-view forecast for any product. The latest retained Clarity baseline measured 24 human sessions across 26 measured products in three days ending 2026-09-04. It is a partial, older session snapshot, not current pageviews or a full-portfolio total. Multiplying a sitemap URL count by an assumed view rate is a scenario, not demand evidence.

For scale, a million **in 12 months** requires about 83,333 views/month. A million for **each** of 42 public products would require about 3.5 million views/month in aggregate. These are target rates, not predictions; sessions, pageviews, video plays, and AI citations must not be added together as though they were the same metric.

## Best bets, in order

1. **Useful corpus pages with real search intent.** SWE Interview Prep, Research Papers, Reddit Insights, Meme Lab, and song worlds have material that could support many distinct public answers. Verify search demand and indexing for a small sample first. Rights and originality are gates for the meme, Reddit, and song assets. More URLs alone do not create views.
2. **Demonstrations distributed where the audience already watches.** The Daddy utilities, native apps, games, and Mashup need a short, verifiable task or gameplay clip. This creates off-site reach and can drive qualified visits; it does not count as an owned-site pageview. App Store search is a channel only for apps actually available there.
3. **Evidence pages for expensive problems.** CodeVetter, PostTrainLLM, App Health, MentionPilot, RolePatch, and Karte can win narrower buyer-intent searches and citations with real examples, comparisons, and proof. Their ceiling may be lower than entertainment/media, but visitors can be much more valuable.
4. **AI and GEO as amplification.** Use AI to help prepare checked explanations and formats from original evidence, then measure model mentions and citations. Technical agent-readiness fixes remove barriers; they do not manufacture recommendations. Google says no special AI markup is needed for its AI search features.
5. **Selective directories and launches.** Use one relevant venue at a time, with destination-specific copy and a live referral receipt. Current accreditation is zero, and Google explicitly treats low-quality directory links created for rankings as spam. A listing burst is unlikely to be the million-view engine.

The four checks for **every row** are:

1. **SEO:** Can a public page answer a real non-brand question, be crawled and indexed, and show a useful next step? The 2026-09-27 homepage SEO audit is a technical baseline, not proof of ranking.
2. **AI:** Can AI help turn a real product result, corpus record, or authored finding into a checked page, demo, clip, or answer without inventing evidence or publishing duplicate thin content? Also check that the final public text is readable by agents. The 2026-09-27 agent-index audit measures protocol availability, not AI recommendations.
3. **GEO:** Is there a source-backed answer worth citing, and does a fixed buyer-question panel actually mention/cite the project? Google requires ordinary indexability and helpful content for AI Overviews; special AI files are not a ranking shortcut. The latest seven-surface model panel is dated 2026-09-05; other projects remain unmeasured there.
4. **Directories:** Is there one audience-fit destination with a verified live submission path, accurate copy, and a unique audience? The local accreditation register currently has zero accredited destinations. No bulk submission is ready to run. Low-quality directory links created mainly for rankings are link spam under Google's policy, so "directory spamming" is excluded.

The action below is the **first useful artifact or decision for growth**, not a claim that a technical SEO fix alone will produce traffic. "Draft" means it can be prepared locally. "External" requires a real account, venue fit, and live-result verification. Actions that involve personal or licensed content remain gated by those facts. Existing third-party and public-route claims must be rechecked before publication.

## Execution and measurement

The 2026-09-27 local homepage SEO audit covers all 42 current public identities. It found critical metadata failures on DaddyRad, Every Song Is a Website, Meme Lab, MentionPilot, Nomad Data Adventure, and Formula Composition Engine; another agent is already editing most of those checkouts. The same-day agent-index audit puts 29/42 at S, 12/42 at A, and one at B. These checks are technical readiness, not traffic outcomes.

Three independent agent-readiness defects were fixed in clean local repositories during this review: Research Papers' canonical agent-catalog name and HEAD parity, Open Historia's HEAD parity, and Memory Map's two missing Markdown target declarations. None is a measured traffic gain or a live change.

The directory registry is a seed list, not a set of approved submission targets: zero destinations are accredited. For each product, verify one niche-specific venue, avoid duplicate community posts, and record a live listing URL plus referring traffic before doing another. Directory submissions are secondary to a useful product/page and its distribution.

The first measured portfolio cohort is SWE Interview Prep, Research Papers, and Meme Lab. Their live Search Console and available Clarity baseline, plus CodeVetter and PostTrainLLM comparison reads, are recorded in `../../site-health/docs/discovery-bottleneck-2026-09-28.md`. The cohort showed very little ordinary Google traffic; it did not identify a proven page to edit for a near-term click gain. The next experiment should be one deliberately scoped content engine, one developer proof asset (CodeVetter), and one native utility demo (StorageDaddy or BrowserDaddy). Compare 28-day external human pageviews, non-brand search clicks, qualified activations, and return visits against the same metrics before publication. Expand only the mechanism that actually moves them. No unobserved increase is credited to these proposals.
