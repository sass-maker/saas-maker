# Questions before accepting any intensive program
- Is admission the same event as investment approval?
- When is cash legally committed and when is it wired?
- Is support company investment, a personal stipend, reimbursement, housing, meals, travel, or credits?
- Can company funds legally and responsibly pay a founder salary?
- What are the equity, SAFE, MFN, discount, program-fee, pro-rata and follow-on rights?
- What hours and physical presence are mandatory?
- What visa or work authorization is required, and is sponsorship offered?
- What expenses remain self-funded?
- Can participation end without investment?
- Can the program invest into an Indian entity, or is another jurisdiction required?
Applications are useful when they create optionality, signal, relationships, capital, credits or better judgment. They become procrastination when they replace customer evidence and distribution.
