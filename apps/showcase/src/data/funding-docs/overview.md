# Sorting logic
- Full-time after funding is a preference, not an eligibility rule. Programs that require self-funded or conditionally funded participation remain visible and can still be worth applying to for brand, network, or optionality.
- Brand-only applications are valid. The directory has a dedicated Brand & Prestige view.
- Free credits and grants are valid opportunities. They have a dedicated view with eligibility, nominal value, caveats, and relevance.
- Fit and recommendation are separate. A program can be prestigious but premature, relevant but self-funded, or low-brand but highly practical.
- One row means one specific program. Different programs from Antler, Afore, Techstars, Google, LAUNCH, and others are not collapsed into organization-level rows.
- Uncertainty is preserved. Stale, secondary-only, inactive, and unidentified items remain searchable instead of being silently deleted or presented as fact.
