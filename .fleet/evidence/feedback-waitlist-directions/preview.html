02 / Direct line

A compact product contact center

Job: provide one consistent entry point across dense apps and marketing pages. A restrained corner trigger opens a side sheet with explicit Updates, Feedback, and Bug states. Clean sans type and green status cues borrow from product verification.

Risk: a shared overlay can compete with an app’s own navigation; placement and mobile safe areas need careful per-product checks.

03 / Open channel

A visible two-action invitation

Job: make both following and feedback hard to miss on expressive consumer pages. Layered cards, a vivid signal color, and a separate feedback tile keep the two intents distinct. Inline by default; the feedback tile opens the existing form.

Risk: playful geometry may overwhelm serious or native-adjacent products; it needs strict brand adaptation rather than one universal skin.
